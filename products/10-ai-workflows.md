# 10 AI Workflows for Marketing Agencies

**Transform Your Agency Operations with Intelligent Automation**

---

## Workflow 1: AI-Powered Lead Scoring

**Description:**  
Automatically analyze and score incoming leads based on engagement, demographics, and behavior patterns. AI evaluates lead quality in real-time, helping your sales team focus on high-value prospects.

**Implementation:**  
- Connect CRM to AI scoring model
- Define scoring criteria (engagement, budget, industry)
- Automate lead routing based on scores
- Track conversion rates to refine model

**Time Saved:** 5-8 hours/week

---

## Workflow 2: Automated Content Creation

- Schedule automated publishing

**Time Saved:** 10-15 hours/week
