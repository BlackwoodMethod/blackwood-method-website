## Stage 1: Lead Capture

### Automation Points

**AI Chatbot:**
- Greets website visitors
- Asks qualifying questions
- Captures contact information
- Schedules initial calls

**Web Forms:**
- Smart form fields
- Progressive profiling
- Auto-response emails
- CRM integration

**Email Inquiry:**
- Auto-acknowledgment
- Sentiment analysis
- Priority routing
- Follow-up scheduling

---

## Stage 2: AI Qualification

### Scoring Criteria

| Factor | Weight | Scoring |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Budget fit | 30% | 0-30 points |
| Service need | 25% | 0-25 points |
| Timeline | 20% | 0-20 points |
| Decision authority | 15% | 0-15 points |
| Company size | 10% | 0-10 points |

**Total Score:**
- 80-100: Hot lead (immediate outreach)
- 60-79: Warm lead (personalized follow-up)
- 40-59: Cool lead (nurture campaign)
- 0-39: Poor fit (polite decline)

### AI Actions

- Analyze form responses
- Score lead automatically
- Route to appropriate workflow
- Trigger next step

---

## Stage 3: Nurture vs. Sales Track

### Nurture Campaign (Low Score)

**Week 1:**
- Welcome email with resources
- Educational content
- Case study

**Week 2:**
- Industry insights
- Free tool/template
- Webinar invitation

**Week 3:**
- Success stories
- ROI calculator
- Re-qualification check

**Week 4:**
- Personal outreach
- Special offer
- Schedule call

### Sales Outreach (High Score)

**Day 1:**
- Personal email from account exec
- Meeting invitation
- Preliminary proposal

**Day 3:**
- Follow-up if no response
- Additional resources
- Alternative meeting times

**Day 7:**
- Final outreach attempt
- Move to nurture if no response

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Over-automation:** Keep human touch at key moments  
❌ **Poor data quality:** Garbage in, garbage out  
❌ **No fallback:** Always have manual override  
❌ **Ignoring feedback:** Listen to leads and clients  
❌ **Set and forget:** Automation needs maintenance
